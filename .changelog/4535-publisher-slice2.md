- The HEAD look and the bash write path now publish one `DiskChange` (`clients/disk-changes.ts`) instead of calling the LSP service and driving the held-primary send and the detached drift pass themselves. `sendToHeldPrimaries` and its drift call moved into the publisher's LSP consumer (`clients/disk-change-subscribers.ts`), so neither detector names a consumer, and a publish that reaches no LSP subscriber records one bounded `disk-change-lsp-consumer-missing` row per root instead of reading as "no held document was stale". A publish whose LSP consumer throws answers `failed: true` with `held: -1` instead of a clean zero. A detector's listed population is published in `DISK_CHANGE_MAX_RECORDED_PATHS` chunks (`publishDiskChangeChunked`), so a code-mod or HEAD delta over more files than the cap keeps its listing: the awaited primary send is drawn from the change it named instead of from every document the root holds (`changedSent 0/65`, `unrelatedSent 64/135` before the fix), and the recovered set's detached resync records a chunk whose drift pass rejects and goes on to the next chunk. A same-HEAD discard (`git stash`, `git checkout .`, `git restore`) now resyncs the documents it reverted (#4502): the command's own baseline records the dirty set before it runs, the `tool_result` compares it with the status after, and the difference is published as a `head-move` change carrying `sources: ["same-head-discard"]` before the result returns. Parallel bash calls whose HEAD looks join one flight each publish their own discard set, and a staged rename that a discard reverts resyncs its restored source side as well as its destination. A throwing out-of-band LSP consumer is reported as `restarted: -1` instead of a clean zero. This is slice 2 of #4535 (detector migration); the consumer census, the staleness markers, the settled sweep, the drift heartbeat and the MCP route follow in slice 3.
