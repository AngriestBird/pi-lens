---
section: Changed
---

- **A cascade now sizes its neighbour walk against the settle time it has left, instead of always walking 40 (refs [#1462](https://github.com/apmantza/pi-lens/issues/1462))** — the flat neighbour cap and the 5000 ms turn-end settle wait were set in two different files with nothing relating them, and dogfood on 2026-08-15 measured what that costs: 50 cascades, median 30 ms, max 3.88 s at 40 neighbours, and a `reverse_deps_cache` refresh ahead of the graph build (2046 ms for `logger.ts`) put both tail runs past the cap. `cascade_settle_wait` then returned `settled: 0` and the run delivered nothing at all, so the flat cap spent its whole budget on exactly the high-fanout files least likely to land in time. Both knobs now live in `clients/cascade-budget.ts` with the derivation that ties them together: after the graph build, the reverse-deps refresh and the LSP-reference expansion — the prelude that buys the overruns — the run charges its own age against the settle window and sizes the rest of the walk from what is left, at the measured ~97 ms marginal cost of one more neighbour under LSP contention. The flat 40 is now the ceiling, never exceeded and untouched while time is ample: the median 30 ms cascade and a 1000 ms prelude both still get the full 40, so nothing narrows until the flat walk genuinely no longer fits. Pressure narrows the walk, it never starves it — a fully spent window still walks the floor (`cascadeMaxFiles`, 5), which covers the measured median fan-out of 1 and p50 of the whole population, so a late cascade returns a short answer rather than a false clean leaf or nothing at all. The derived budget also caps the transitive BFS, so a late run expands less as well as walking less. Modelled against the issue's own timings, three of seven representative scenarios overran the window under the flat cap and one does after the change — the remaining one is a run whose prelude alone exceeds the whole window, where the floor deliberately wins. [#1446](https://github.com/apmantza/pi-lens/issues/1446)'s `cascade_result` telemetry carries the evidence: `neighborBudget` is now the budget actually in force, alongside the new `neighborBudgetCeiling` it was narrowed from and `budgetRemainingMs` that decided it, with `budgetTruncated` counted against the effective budget. Tunable through `PI_LENS_CASCADE_NEIGHBOUR_COST_MS` and `PI_LENS_CASCADE_NEIGHBOUR_FLOOR`; setting `PI_LENS_CASCADE_SETTLE_WAIT_MS=0` disables the settle wait and restores the full flat cap, since a run that never blocks turn_end has no window to fit inside.
